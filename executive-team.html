Chicago Asantefuohemaa

Nana Afia Durowaa Nhyira

Born in Pankrono, Kumasi, of the Aduana clan, she settled in Chicago in 1998 and found her place in Asanteman two years later. Called to queenship in 2007, she served as Akwamuhemaa for eight years and then as Ohemaa Abediakyire before taking the stool of Asantefuohemaa.

Members know her for a calm, motherly way of leading, and for teaching the community’s children the Adowa dance so the tradition passes on.

From her profile in Adehye, the 2026 inauguration magazine.
