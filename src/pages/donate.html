AACM is a tax-exempt 501(c)(3) non-profit organization. Its work includes donations of hospital equipment and books to Ghana, cultural events and programs in Chicago, and support for Ghanaians settling in the Midwest.

HealthcareSeventeen hospital beds and ten dialysis machines for Komfo Anokye Teaching Hospital, Kumasi.
EducationFive thousand books for the Ashanti Public Library & Cultural Center.
Culture & communityFestivals, Akwasidae gatherings and guidance for newcomers.
