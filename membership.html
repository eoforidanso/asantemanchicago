Individuals & families

Individual Membership

Membership in the Asanteman Association of Chicago & Midwest (AACM) offers individuals and families the opportunity to connect, serve, and preserve Asante culture and heritage while supporting meaningful community, educational, and humanitarian initiatives in the United States and Ghana.

As an AACM member, you become part of a supportive network dedicated to unity, leadership, and service within the Ghanaian and African diaspora community across Chicago and the Midwest. Members enjoy opportunities for cultural engagement, community involvement, networking, and contributing to projects that create lasting impact.

Join AACM today and be part of a growing community committed to strengthening Asanteman at home and abroad.

Businesses & organizations

Corporate Partnerships

The Asanteman Association of Chicago & Midwest (AACM) partners with businesses and organizations to advance cultural preservation, community development, and humanitarian initiatives in the United States and Ghana. Our work is made possible through the support of corporate partners and sponsors who share our commitment to service and impact.

By partnering with AACM, your organization can enhance brand visibility, strengthen corporate social responsibility (CSR) efforts, engage employees, and connect with the Ghanaian and African diaspora community across Chicago and the Midwest — all while supporting meaningful causes.

Join AACM as a corporate partner and help build a stronger, united Asanteman at home and abroad.
