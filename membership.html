Individuals & families

Individual Membership

Membership in the Asanteman Association of Chicago & Midwest (AACM) offers individuals and families the opportunity to connect, serve, and preserve Asante culture and heritage while supporting meaningful community, educational, and humanitarian initiatives in the United States and Ghana.

As an AACM member, you become part of a supportive network dedicated to unity, leadership, and service within the Ghanaian and African diaspora community across Chicago and the Midwest. Members enjoy opportunities for cultural engagement, community involvement, networking, and contributing to projects that create lasting impact.

Join AACM today and be part of a growing community committed to strengthening Asanteman at home and abroad.

Businesses & organizations

Corporate Partnerships

The Asanteman Association of Chicago & Midwest (AACM) partners with businesses and organizations to advance cultural preservation, community development, and humanitarian initiatives in the United States and Ghana. Our work is made possible through the support of corporate partners and sponsors who share our commitment to service and impact.

By partnering with AACM, your organization can enhance brand visibility, strengthen corporate social responsibility (CSR) efforts, engage employees, and connect with the Ghanaian and African diaspora community across Chicago and the Midwest — all while supporting meaningful causes.

Join AACM as a corporate partner and help build a stronger, united Asanteman at home and abroad.

Membership FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can’t find what you need? Write to info@chicagoasanteman.org.

What is the Asanteman Association of Chicago & Midwest?

AACM is a non-profit cultural and social organization founded to unite and support individuals of Asante heritage in the Chicago and Midwest region. The association promotes the rich traditions, customs, and values of the Asante people from Ghana while fostering community development, education, and cultural awareness.

Who can join AACM, and how do I become a member?

Membership is open to all individuals of Asante descent, as well as those interested in supporting and preserving Asante culture and heritage. To become a member, you can complete an application form available through the association's leadership or at community events. Membership may include dues and active participation in association activities and initiatives.

What types of events and activities does AACM organize?

AACM hosts cultural, educational, and social events throughout the year. These include traditional celebrations such as the Akwasidae Festival, youth mentorship programs, fundraising for community projects, educational workshops, and social gatherings that strengthen unity among members and support the broader African diaspora community.

How does AACM contribute to the community?

Through scholarships, charitable donations, community outreach programs, and partnerships with other organizations, AACM supports education, healthcare, and social empowerment initiatives both locally and in Ghana.
